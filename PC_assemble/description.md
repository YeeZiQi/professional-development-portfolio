# PC Assemble Session

**Date:** 4 November 2026\
**Activity:** PC Assembly Session

## Activity Description
On 4 November 2026, students from SECP 1513 Section 01 were divided into several groups, 
with each group provided with a computer system unit and a screwdriver.

Under the guidance of a professional technician, we successfully disassembled and reassembled the computer.

After completing the assembly, we tested the computer to ensure that all components were
properly reconnected and the computer was functioning correctly.
