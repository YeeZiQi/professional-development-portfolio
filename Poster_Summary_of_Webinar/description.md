# Assignment 2 : Poster on Industry Talk

## From Developer to CEO: The Perfect Blend of Technology and Business 
### Introduction
This project is based on TIS Webinar - Industry Talk 1, delivered by **Assoc. Prof. Dr. Hj. Mohamad Hafis Izran bin Ishak**, 
the CEO of AR Mechatronics PLT,
on 26 October 2026,
from 2 PM to 4 PM.

Dr. Hafis presents ICT leadership as an end-to-end responsibility that extends beyond writing code 
to managing the services surrounding a technological solution.
Drawing from his transition from a UTM researcher to the CEO of AR Mechatronics PLT, the creator of MyREDS,
he highlights the importance of developing practical systems that meet diverse users' needs,
launching a Minimum Viable Product(MVP),
improving products based on real-world feedback,
and ensuring long-term financial sustainability
through cost management and continuous support.

### Activity
Our group created a poster featuring an executive summary, an introduction,
key points from the talk, and reflections on the industry talk.
Through this activity, we gained valuable insights into the relationship between technology and business. 
We learned that writing strong code alone does not guarantee the success of an ICT product.
Instead, developers should also adopt a CEO mindset by
understanding customers' needs and expectations,
delivering practical technological solutions,
and considering the long-term sustainability of their products.
